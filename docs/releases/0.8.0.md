# Halcyon 0.8.0

## New: Data tiles

- **Data tiles** is a new widget for numbers and words that other programs keep up to date. Each of up to six tiles reads a JSON file or a web address on your network, picks one field and shows it as text, a progress bar, a dial or a graph of its recent readings. Start from a template, choose a file and pick the field from the list Halcyon reads out of it. A tile says when its value has not changed for a while or could not be read. Sources stay on this computer and out of shared Looks. Sign-in is not supported, so use addresses without a key in them.

## Widgets

- **System monitor** watches up to six services, system services, containers and ports you choose, in place of the two fixed indicators. Start or restart your own services and containers from its full reading. System services and ports are read only.
- **Markets** charts a week, a month or three months. **Refresh now** asks every source again. Price alerts mark a symbol that crosses the level you set and send one notification, which reaches the Activity island. Alerts stay on this computer.
- **Weather** has a full reading with each current reading, the next 24 hours and the four-day forecast.
- **Folder** has a full reading that lists every entry with its size and date. Open files and folders, open the folder in Files, start a terminal there or copy a path. Subfolders open inside the card, with a way back.
- **Notes** can split into pages at each heading, one page at a time with its own checklist count. Bold, italic, code and numbered lists show on the card. The editor still edits the whole note.
- **Devices** has a full reading with every device, its battery and whether it is charging.
- The **Widget builder** now makes designs for **Notes** and **Devices**, alongside Clock, Weather, Calendar, System monitor, Music and Focus.

## Activity island, edge menu and Settings

- Turn the island's quick timer into a Focus session that keeps its time. Pin Focus, Calendar and Devices from the island, and use Skip, Stop and the other Focus lengths there.
- The edge menu can hold **Play or pause** and **Next track**, which work while the Music widget is hidden. Peek buttons take one of eight icons, and the widgets a Peek shows can be reordered.
- The Widgets page links straight to the **Widget glass** editor and names the glass in use.

## Command line

- `halcyon --styles` and `halcyon --style NAME` list and apply the styles from Appearance. `halcyon --looks` and `halcyon --look NAME` switch Looks. `halcyon-ctl` has the same commands: `styles`, `style`, `looks` and `look`.
- `halcyon-ctl timer focus` turns a running quick timer into a Focus session.
- `halcyon-ctl` says when Halcyon isn't running and exits with an error instead of doing nothing. `toggle` takes every widget and panel `halcyon --toggle` takes.
