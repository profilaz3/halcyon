# Halcyon 0.7.0

- **Looks, part by part:** a look is now a whole desktop: panel and dock, window frame, colors, fonts and icons, widget style and layout, and a wallpaper with its weather effect. Before you apply one, pick the parts you want and the preview shows exactly those parts, with **Now** and **With this look** to compare. Apply writes only those parts, and Undo takes them back.
- **Look studio:** **New look from my desktop**, from a plain look or from the selected look opens the **Look studio**, with a live preview on top and one picture per part below. Take each part from your desktop, from any look or from the part's own choices, then save, duplicate or rename the result in your library. Harbor is the first handcrafted look.
- **Rainy Lake and Deep Purple:** two new ready-made themes on the **Themes** page, each with a matching look: Rainy Lake with the bundled Morning fog wallpaper, rain and the clock behind the mountains, and Deep Purple with the Halcyon night wallpaper.
- **Fonts and icons:** **Fonts and icons** has its own sidebar entry. Icon themes show as cards with real sample icons, and **Get more icons** browses and installs icon packs from the Pling catalog, unpacked safely into your own icon folder. **App icon shape** turns app icons into circles, squircles or rounded squares, and folders can take your accent color. Looks remember the icon theme, the shape and the folder color.
- **Edge menu:** the screen edge menu has its own page under **Widgets**. Turn it off, move it to the left or right edge at any height, make it shorter, open it on hover or only on click, and show it as a list, a slim column of icons or a grid. You choose what it lists: widget switches, Halcyon actions, looks, apps and folders, separators and section titles. Every change applies live.
- **Settings Home:** Settings now opens on **Home**, a picture of your desktop built from your real setup. Point at a part to light it up and click to open its page. The sidebar follows the parts of the desktop, from **Wallpaper** and **Effects** to **Panel**, **Dock** and **Activity island**. Previews show the real bars and widgets, and only the parts that belong to the page you are on respond to clicks. The setup wizard shows the dock and panel close up too.
- **Arrange matches the desktop:** **Arrange widgets** now opens over the real widgets on your desktop, drawn exactly as the desktop draws them, so nothing shifts or gets cut off. Add a widget with **Add widget**, remove one, or open its settings without leaving Arrange, and Undo or Cancel bring a removed widget back where it was. **Arrange with keyboard** stays in Settings.
- **The clock behind the mountains, in the rain:** with rain on, the desktop clock now sits behind the glass, so drops bend it and fog softens it, and on scenes like Ridge it stays behind the mountains with every weather effect, even with a top panel. Rain fogs the window, and drops bead, merge, slide down winding paths and leave trails that slowly fog over again. Snow no longer shimmers. The effects have their own **Effects** page, which `halcyon-ctl settings --page effects` opens.
- **Rain that rests:** rain, snow, fog and clouds hold still on a display that is covered by a maximized or fullscreen window, turned off or idle, and carry on from the same moment when you see them again. A rain surface that stopped drawing now restarts by itself instead of freezing.
- **Activity island:** notifications from one app group into one row, a **Recent** list keeps what you dismissed this session, and each app can be muted, always shown or kept in the list only. Live activities show copy and download progress, a quick timer (also `halcyon-ctl timer 10`) and music with a scrubber and an output picker. Drop files or links on the island to keep them on its **File shelf**, and pick an **Island style**: follow the theme or Dark.
- **Devices and battery:** the island tells you when headphones, a mouse or a phone connect, and warns at 20% and again at 10% battery. A new **Devices** widget, off by default, shows a battery ring for each device. Readings come from UPower, BlueZ, KDE Connect and GSConnect, and your laptop's own battery is left out.
- **Monitor graphs and Music:** CPU, GPU and network history in System monitor are now area charts whose scale follows the load, and Memory and Disk always show bars. When nothing plays, the Music card keeps the last track with **Resume**.
- **Upgrades:** after an update, the widgets and the Activity island restart into the new version on their own once nothing on the desktop is in use, and programs you opened from a widget keep running.
- **Lighter on your computer:** fewer panel restarts and less work while idle. Settings marks the controls that restart the panel with **This restarts the panel**, and changing to a style with the same bar spacing no longer restarts it. The Activity island draws with a lighter renderer and uses far less memory, and the island and the widgets no longer poll the system bus, the session or the calendar: they follow only the changes they show. The clock behind the scene no longer rereads the wallpaper every minute, applying a change writes faster, and the Islands panel helper no longer wakes every 20 ms.
- **Safer settings, Apply and Undo:** a value in the settings file that Halcyon can't use, such as an unknown choice or a number out of range, no longer resets everything. Only that value falls back, the rest is kept, and Home names what was set aside. Applying a look always finishes, with a note when the Islands panel can't take it yet, and Undo keeps the colors it restored instead of letting automatic wallpaper colors repaint them. Every time Halcyon shows follows the **24-hour time** switch.
- **Usage data:** reports under **Share usage data** now use usage schema 8, which adds the Devices widget and page to the widgets and pages a report can name. Nothing else changed. [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md) lists every field.
- **Fixes:** wallpapers picked in Halcyon show again on COSMIC 1.11, which keeps separate wallpapers for light and dark mode. Turning off Daily overview no longer leaves the desktop without a clock, **Show the date** off hides the date in every clock style, a slideshow folder no longer keeps reloading on the Wallpaper page, the Islands autostart stays quiet once the Islands files are removed, and installing from source keeps only the two newest program backups.
