# Halcyon 0.6.5

- **Match the weather:** rain, snow, fog and cloud shadows on your wallpaper can now follow the Weather widget, fading in and out as the weather changes and never stronger than your own settings. Turn on **Match the weather** under **Moving effect**, choose a **Weather strength** (Subtle, Natural or Strong), and use **Adjust** to tune snow before it snows.
- **Clock stage:** the Clock page now shows a small copy of your screen with the clock at its real place and size. Drag the clock to move it, drag its corner to resize it, or use the arrow keys and the plus and minus keys, and each move is one Undo step.
- **The scene covers the clock:** **Let the scene cover the clock** now works on your own wallpapers as well as Ridge, and the new **Scene lighting** (Off, Soft or Rich) tints the digits with the light of the sky behind them. For your own pictures, press **Download** on the Clock page to get the scene model once (about 99 MB). Pictures are checked on your computer and never uploaded.
- **Islands setup:** pick **Islands** under **Dock and panel → Shape** and Halcyon shows one clear next step, such as **Sign out now** to finish switching, **Try again** with the real reason, or **Undo**. Reinstalling Halcyon no longer blocks turning Islands on, and when the Islands panel is missing or unavailable, Settings and the setup wizard say why and how to get it back.
- **Changes:** **Changes** now sits at the bottom of the Settings sidebar and lists your history by day, with names like "Changed Clock" or "Moved Time to Right" and an **Open** button that takes you to the setting. You can undo any change on its own, and the footer **Undo** and Ctrl+Z only act on the page where you made the change.
- **Time of day:** one **Time of day** page now switches either your whole look or just the widget style for Day, Evening and Night. Until you choose a city, it asks for that one thing first.
- **Settings, tidied up:** explanations now sit behind a small **?** beside each setting, or turn on **Show explanations** under **Halcyon → Interface** to see them all. The General page is now called **Halcyon**, **Running and startup** shows whether Halcyon is running with **Start** or **Stop**, **Accessibility** has its own sidebar entry, **Appearance** has one **Corners** row for widgets, bars and windows, and the Dock and Panel share one **Items** editor.
- **Search and names:** search now finds settings by the names you see on screen, including partial words and common synonyms. Names are consistent too: saved setups are now **looks** (keep yours with **Save current look…**), the Activity island is now the **Activity pill**, and the screen-edge menu is now **Widget controls**.
- **Keyboard and screen readers:** every switch, slider and dropdown in Settings now works from the keyboard and has a name a screen reader can say, and Escape closes one thing at a time. **Accessibility** has a new **Keyboard** list of shortcuts.
- **Reduce motion and readable text:** when animations are turned off in COSMIC, Halcyon now stops rain, the visualizer, the Activity pill animation and the Widget controls slide. Glass and Frosted cards are more opaque so text stays readable, small text is larger, and a see-through clock that is hard to read offers **Use a card**.
- **First run and updates:** until setup is done, Home shows one **Set up your desktop** card, and skipping the welcome no longer counts as finished. After an update, Home shows a short **Halcyon was updated** card, and **See what's new** lists every release since the one you last saw. If Settings was open during an update, it offers **Restart Settings**.
- **On the desktop:** right-click a widget for its settings, **Arrange widgets** or **Hide**, notes save when you click away, and the Music card shrinks to one line when nothing is playing. If something can't be saved, Halcyon now says so in plain words with **Try again** and **Details**, and starting Halcyon twice no longer doubles your widgets.
- **Lighter on your computer:** Halcyon no longer uses most of a CPU core while idle with the Islands panel connected, and no longer re-reads your wallpaper picture every 30 seconds. Video memory use dropped from about 370 MB to about 40 MB on a test machine, because hidden widgets no longer keep windows open and widget windows no longer reserve memory they don't use.
- **Fixes:** the corner, outline and shadow controls on the **Windows** page now take effect and save. The Calendar page no longer crashes when Google sign-in becomes available, a panel that hides itself no longer pushes widgets around as it slides in, and Settings pages fit better in narrow windows.
