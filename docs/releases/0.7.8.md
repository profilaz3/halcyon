# Halcyon 0.7.8

## Widgets

- **Markets** follows up to five stocks and currency pairs. Public stock quotes and European Central Bank exchange rates work without an account. Daily changes, a price chart, publication dates and source details can be switched individually. The watchlist fits the card when resized and keeps the last successful quotes if a refresh fails. Twelve Data remains available for stocks. [Markets setup](../MARKETS.md)
- **Folder** opens recent files from a local directory in their usual apps. A narrow widget shows a list. A wider one adds tiles and image thumbnails. The folder path stays on the local machine when sharing a Look.
- **Widget Peek** brings a chosen set of widgets over the current window. Add **Peek widgets** in the edge menu editor to choose the widgets and button icon. Use the same button or shortcut, Escape, or a click outside to dismiss it.
- **Music** can cast a soft glow from the album cover. **Weather** offers Classic and Outlook layouts, with a side-by-side preview in Settings. **Messages** fits more senders when message previews are hidden.
- The command-line controller can open the new widget settings directly. Run `halcyon-ctl settings --page markets` or `halcyon-ctl settings --page folder`.

## Appearance, dock and workspaces

- **Glass finishes** let widgets share a named material or use their own copy. Adjust frost, wallpaper refraction, tint and corner shape. The default tint follows the theme. Crystal bends wallpaper details around the rim and reflects their color. Its clear center stays aligned behind the text at fractional and integer display scales. Rain adds moving droplets. [Native Crystal screenshot](../screenshots/desktop-customization/crystal-feather.webp) · [Glass comparisons](../screenshots/078/README.md)
- **Mica Slate, Tape Deck, Porcelain and Blueprint** coordinate widgets, dock and panel. Existing and personal styles stay available. The Appearance preview shows which surfaces a choice changes.
- **Dock magnification** smoothly enlarges the icon under the pointer by up to 45%. Neighbors grow less, launchers keep their click targets, and system reduced motion keeps icons still. Enable it in **Dock → Dock magnification → Apply**.
- **Workspace launchers** pair a saved Look with an ordered list of apps. In **Looks**, select a saved Look, choose its apps, then choose **Start workspace**. Saving, importing or scheduling the Look leaves the apps closed.

## Activity island, navigation and reliability

- Home and Activity island show the next step in Islands activation, including when another sign-in is needed. The Activity island can also run on the standard COSMIC panel. Pin Music or Messages from the island, place it with the pointer or arrow keys, or locate an existing widget with **Show on desktop**.
- Widget content adapts to its saved size. Open the full agenda, project list or system reading in a resizable window. From the edge menu, **Shift+Enter** focuses an interactive widget or opens its full reading. Keyboard focus, back navigation and search destinations have been improved.
- Calendar distinguishes loading, disconnected and empty calendars. Open readings keep their data providers active. Changing the selected Markets symbol keeps the original refresh deadline, and Settings preserves unfinished watchlist edits.
- The Activity island returns from Devices and clears expired notifications. The edge menu checks apps and folders in the background, and its controls follow the display where the menu opened. Settings keeps search across window resizes and returns to the original destination after setup.
