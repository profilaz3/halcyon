# Halcyon 0.5.0

- Islands is included in the main package. Enable it in Dock & panel; app and panel updates arrive together.
- Penumbra fades dock and panel backgrounds into the wallpaper, with separate controls for each bar. Thanks to [motixo](https://github.com/motixo/penumbra-panel).
- Switch appearances at dawn, noon, golden hour and other solar events, calculated locally for your chosen city.
- Adaptive Tidal, Paper and Moss looks change through the day.
- Build personal widgets with Calendar agendas and selected system readings.
- Clearer onboarding, setup review, layout editing, Calendar guidance and change history. Hiding widgets preserves your selections.

Download `cosmic-ext-halcyon_0.5.0_amd64.deb`, or install the update through COSMIC Store. Existing Islands installations migrate automatically; enabled panel updates take effect at your next login.
