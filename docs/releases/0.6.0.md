# Halcyon 0.6.0

- **Activity island (beta):** music, Focus sessions, upcoming meetings, notifications, Bluetooth devices and updates in the middle of the top panel. Green and orange dots show when the camera or microphone is in use, and Do Not Disturb is one switch away. Needs the Islands panel style. Add it in **Dock and panel → Panel**. This is a beta: tell us what works and what does not.
- **Rain, snow and fog setups:** Rainy evening, First snow and Morning fog each bring their own wallpaper, colors and weather effect.
- **Tidier built-in setups:** the time and weather sit together at the top left, with the system monitor and music on the right. Moss is now part of Tidal.
- **Much more see-through:** widgets can be up to 90% see-through; the dock and panel up to 55%.
- **Settings, rearranged:** organized by what you change, Back returns where you came from, and each widget has Content, Design and Position tabs.
- **What's new:** these notes are also in **Settings → Updates**, with buttons that open each feature.
