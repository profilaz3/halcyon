# Halcyon 0.5.5

- GPU wallpaper effects: rain, snow, fog and cloud shadows, with adjustable density, size, speed and quality. Effects pause on lock and sleep; battery pause is on by default.
- Customizable Music and Focus widgets, with compact and wide layouts.
- Circular Music visualizers and an audio spectrum with adjustable size, placement and opacity, including behind the dock.
- Native Fedora 43 RPM package. Halcyon, Islands and Penumbra update together through the system updater.
