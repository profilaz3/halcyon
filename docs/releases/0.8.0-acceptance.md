# Halcyon 0.8.0 release candidate review

## Changes reviewed

The comparison starts at the published 0.7.8 tag. The candidate includes the widget, Activity island, Settings and command-line work in PRs #134 to #148, and the Outlook time zone fix in #133. PRs #142 to #148 overlapped in the Notes card, the saved widget fields and shared tests. They were merged in order, and each step on main was compile-checked before the next. PR #128 concerns the separately deployed public reports website. [Feature notes](0.8.0.md)

## Before publication

Packages for Pop!_OS 24.04 and Fedora 43 and 44 are built from this commit and tested for fresh installation and upgrade from the published 0.7.8 packages. The Islands panel and the magnifying dock are unchanged since 0.7.8 and keep their verified platform binaries. Final publication requires the reviewed commit and CI results, the signed packages and repository metadata, and the final checksums and approval bound to those bytes.
